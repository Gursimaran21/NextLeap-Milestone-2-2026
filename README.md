# NextLeap-Milestone-2-2026

## Milestone 2 - ChatGPT : Why Users are Not using Voice Input on ChatGPT Mobile, especially in India

**Week 3: Problem Framing, Ideation & Validation + Metrics**

### Milestone 2

Your manager is happy with your market understanding and wants you to go deeper.

Your task over the next 2 weeks is to understand why users are not using voice input on ChatGPT mobile, especially in India.

You need to:

1. Lay out 4-5 user segments who are likely to use ChatGPT mobile (e.g., students, job seekers, content creators, regional language users).

2. Choose one user segment to focus on - ideally the one with the highest potential impact.

3. Write down hypotheses on why this segment is not using voice input.

4. Design a short survey and collect 30+ responses from that segment.

5. Talk to 5-6 users in that segment to understand real behaviour, anxieties, and mental models.

6. Based on the insights, fill in the Problem Framing Canvas to clearly define the real barrier to voice usage.

You need to submit a 3-slide deck:

Slide 1: Chosen segment and justification of impact

Slide 2: Insights from user research (quantitative and qualitative) - pain points, blockers

Slide 3: Problem Framing Canvas
