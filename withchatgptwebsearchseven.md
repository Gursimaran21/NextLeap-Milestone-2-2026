### Solution to ChatGPT Problem Framing, Ideation & Validation + Metrics: Why Users are Not using Voice Input on ChatGPT Mobile, especially in India

#### Task 7 - Based on the insights, fill in the Problem Framing Canvas to clearly define the real barrier to voice usage

**Problem Framing Canvas — ChatGPT Voice Adoption in India**

Based on the research we've done, I would **reframe the problem away from “students don't know Voice exists.**” OpenAI identifies India as its largest student market for ChatGPT, with millions using it for homework, exam prep and exploration. Recent India-focused research also suggests that awareness can be relatively high while **regular Voice usage remains low**, pointing toward trust, context and workflow friction.

**1. 👤 Target User**

**Mobile-first Indian students aged 18–24**

- College/university students
- Exam and competitive-test aspirants
- Early-career learners
- Frequent ChatGPT users
- Often multilingual: English + Hindi/Hinglish/regional language
- Frequently use ChatGPT in hostels, homes, libraries, campuses and while commuting

This is a high-value segment because India has the world's largest student population on ChatGPT, and OpenAI is actively investing in India-focused learning experiences.
